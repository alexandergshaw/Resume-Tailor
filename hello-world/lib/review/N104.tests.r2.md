### Why the modal async-safety lives in a controller + a reachability render (not one giant mount test)
The double-fire guard, the atomic replace, the one-level Undo and the "a late result is tagged to its
own job" guard are CONCURRENCY, which cannot be proven synchronously (agent rule 13): they are proven in
`lib/tailor/regenerateJob.js` with DEFERRED promises the test settles after the second click / tab switch
has already happened - the faithful home UXW-5/UXW-8 names ("a new lib/tailor/ module the thin mount hook
wraps"). The one property that can only be proven on the production path - that the control is actually
ON SCREEN and correctly gated by job kind + engine (the "panel with no opening button" failure this seat
exists to prevent) - is proven by rendering the REAL `DocumentPreviewMount` (rule 2). I did NOT invent a
mount-internal entry point or force an export to reach anything (rule 6).
