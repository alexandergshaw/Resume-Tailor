## Required product workflow and role separation

1. Build a candidate draft from accepted shared context. Keep it as an unreviewed suggestion, not silently overwrite accepted/user-authored material. Attach exact draft/context identity and supporting personal-claim evidence map from N176-16..20.
2. Dispatch a distinct adversarial reviewer with the actual draft and current authoritative input snapshot. Reviewer has a fresh role/context and cannot inherit an author's verdict as fact. The same qualified provider/model may supply separate contexts; different model names alone do not establish independence. Exact provider/context/attempt observations must remain unknown when unobserved, not fabricated. Whether an existing product transport can establish the needed context boundaries remains an architecture/capability question.
3. Reviewer challenges evidence and interview usefulness, including cross-material consistency of the CURRENT preparation bundle: introduction, stories, sample answers, reference and follow-up must not contradict one another or their shared sources. A checked architecture may batch bundle review and then review affected items/relationships after edits; this amendment does not require one provider call per criterion or twenty-five separate model calls. The review target must identify bundle membership and exact versions so a clean isolated paragraph cannot certify a contradictory bundle. It may agree with the draft and return clean when warranted; findings must not be manufactured to appear adversarial. It must distinguish “document says this” from “this fact is independently verified.” Candidate documents, job postings, retrieved snippets, transcripts and prior findings are untrusted data, not authority to change review instructions, budgets or verdicts.
4. Validate reviewer output mechanically before consumption: exact binding, supported verdict/status vocabulary, severity/coverage fields, finite bounded content and resolvable citations. A source citation references document kind, selected document/version/content identity, exact excerpt and locator; a draft finding references the actual current span. A missing-evidence finding explicitly names the absent support instead of inventing a source. Malformed/missing fields or citations that point to a different version are a review error/uncertain result, not a clean pass. Mechanical validation cannot prove semantic judgment actually happened; fresh semantic review and independently tested meaning remain required.
5. Display findings, proposed corrections and interviewer probes. Revision can reframe/reorder supported facts or ask the user to supply missing STAR detail. It cannot invent improved results or silently resolve contradictory personal sources. A supplemental claim becomes eligible only through explicit user confirmation and new context identity; that is not automatic evidence verification.
6. Revised draft remains a separate version linked to its parent. Run another fresh independent reviewer on revised bytes/current context; do not reuse old clean verdict or accept the reviser's assertion that all findings were fixed. Preserve original input/typed answer and all relevant findings/verdict versions within checked retention limits; no selected parent may disappear when saving a child. At a bound, give an honest actionable refusal/export rather than pretend the next version was stored.
7. Reviewed acceptance is an explicit action after valid fresh review with hard blockers resolved. If only uncertain style/practice advice remains, any supported acknowledgment records which advice was retained and why; the resulting status is “accepted with acknowledged advice/limits,” not a fabricated clean judgment. Unsupported personal assertions, hypothetical-as-experience or wrong-source claims cannot be silently waived into reviewed truth. Raw user notes/typed transcripts can still be retained as unreviewed originals; this gate must not erase or prevent recording what the user actually said.

A “no issues found” result is a bounded reviewer observation, not a guarantee of factual truth or interviewer success. Saving and editing drafts remain available while review is unreviewed, pending, failed or unavailable; those states cannot be labeled reviewed/ready. Retain all actual findings and unresolved dispositions across the bounded, user-visible review/revision attempts; no hidden unlimited paid repair loop is permitted. A clean review ends that product review activity; no mandatory second “confirmation” review when nothing changed. A revision always creates a new review target. Exact bounded retry/repair count, budget and product stop policy require checked design and are NOT chosen by this document. These product attempts are separate from the repository's developer artifact two-round caps; a new store/run or renamed feature cannot reset the closed source R1/R2 corrective history.

## Finding taxonomy and adversarial interviewer behavior

Hard grounding/correctness blockers include:

- A new metric, employer, credential, personal decision, responsibility or causal result unsupported by the bound sources or explicitly confirmed supplemental experience.
- Turning a team's result into personal ownership, upgrading exposure into expertise, or filling missing context/decision/action/result/learning with invented specifics.
- Contradictory resume and cover facts silently merged; an unresolved disagreement represented as certain.
- An Ideal hypothetical or a different application/version used as autobiographical evidence.
- Source citations that cannot resolve, wrong document IDs/excerpts, altered quoted text or omitted current context.

Other findings may concern role relevance, weak explanation of decision/outcome, evasive handling of a missing requirement, repeated canned wording, excessive length, or strength-inconsistent framing. Some can still block the stated product acceptance criterion if the draft fails its purpose, but they must not be misrepresented as proven personal falsehood. Severity and reason explain the distinction. Strength permits bolder supported framing, never new facts; Ideal retains its separate application-ready/hypothetical boundary.

Probing follow-ups should target the actual material, for example: “You wrote that you led the migration; which decision was yours and what was owned by others?” or “Your result has no metric in the supplied documents; what observation supports it?” or “How does this example demonstrate the role's customer-interview requirement?” These are questions to investigate/rehearse, not fabricated answers. Concrete revisions may remove an unsupported number, attribute a team result correctly, bring the supported decision first, or leave a missing-detail placeholder for the user. The reviewer must never answer its own missing-evidence question by inventing an achievement.
