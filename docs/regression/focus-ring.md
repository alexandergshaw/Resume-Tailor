### R-305 | area: focus-ring | parallel-safe: yes | automatable: no

**Summary:** The app-wide SC 2.4.7 keyboard focus ring (`app/theme/index.js`'s `MuiButtonBase.styleOverrides.root["&.Mui-focusVisible"]` rule) is real and automatically verified for all eleven control types by `app/theme/focusVisible.test.js`, but jsdom has no layout engine, so that file's own header names checks a browser alone can perform -- including one that was a live BLOCKER until this chunk: the ring was clipped to invisibility on every `Tab` in the app (`NavTabs.js`, `DocumentPreviewDialog.js`, `LibraryEditor.js`, `login/page.js`) because `Tabs.js`'s own `TabsRoot`/`TabsScroller` clip anything painted OUTSIDE a tab's border box, and nothing in any of those four call sites leaves padding in that chain to absorb the ring's outward band. `Tab` now rings INSET instead (`MuiTab.styleOverrides.root["&.Mui-focusVisible"].outlineOffset = "-2px"`, `app/theme/index.js`), which no ancestor's `overflow: hidden` can clip. This case is the enumerated, human-run check for that fix plus the browser-only checks the theme's own test file already deferred -- none of it runs in CI, so without this entry none of it is owed to anyone.

**Steps (manual, real browser, Chrome and Firefox, BOTH light and dark theme):**
1. Tab to the app shell's top-level `NavTabs`, to a `Tabs` row inside `DocumentPreviewDialog`, to one inside `LibraryEditor`, and to the one on `/login`. Confirm a full ring appears on all four edges of the focused tab -- not just slivers between adjacent tabs -- and does NOT appear on a mouse click.
2. Click any `Button` with the mouse -- confirm no ring appears; press Tab to reach it -- confirm a 2px solid ring appears, offset OUTWARD from the button's edge.
3. Confirm the ring is not clipped by any other `overflow: hidden` ancestor -- table cells, Accordion summaries, the StatusBar dock -- and is not occluded by the sticky AppHeader or the fixed dock.
4. Confirm none of the app's three read-only `--paper-bg` surfaces (`DocumentPreviewDialog`'s preview page, `CompanyResearchDialog`'s letter preview, `MfaSetupDialog`'s QR) holds a focusable control -- the dark-mode ring measures under 3:1 there and is exempt only because nothing lives on it today.
5. Tab through each of the app's six pre-existing hand-rolled focus rings (SpeakerChip, PageTreeItem, `knowledgePanelStyles.FOCUS_SX`, CopyDocumentControl, DocumentPreviewDialog's editor, `page.module.css`'s `.dupFlagAction`) and confirm none paints two concentric rings.

**Expected:** Every item above holds, no exceptions. Step 1 is the one that used to fail outright: `NavTabs.js` sets `minHeight: 0` on both the `Tabs` root and `.MuiTab-root` with no padding anywhere to absorb the ring, and `Tabs.js` (`TabsRoot`, `:74`; `TabsScroller`, `:118` fixed / `:215-216` scrollable) sets `overflow: hidden` on both -- so the outward 2px-4px band was clipped away entirely, leaving at most a 2px sliver between adjacent tabs and nothing at all on the first, the last, or in the other three Tabs call sites, which carry the identical clipping chain. The inset ring is drawn INSIDE the tab's own border box, so no ancestor `overflow: hidden` can ever reach it.

**Automated coverage this manual case supplements, not duplicates:** `app/theme/focusVisible.test.js` mounts all eleven `ButtonBase`-rooted control types under the app's real theme, in both modes, and asserts from the LIVE cascade (never from source): the ring's four outline longhands at rest and with `Mui-focusVisible` applied by hand (jsdom cannot reliably focus anything); that it is keyed on MUI's own class rather than a focus pseudo-class; that its colour clears 3:1 against every enumerated surface token; that it differs between light and dark (proving it resolves from a real token rather than an unresolved `var()`); and that `Tab` specifically measures `outlineOffset: "-2px"` where every other surface measures `"2px"`. What no jsdom suite can do -- real mouse-vs-keyboard gating, real layout and clipping, real occlusion by a sticky/fixed ancestor, and real concentric-ring perception -- is exactly steps 1 through 5 above.
