**TEST_REFERENCED 368 → 369** against my reference. Cause: my minimal reference stubbed the client consumption
resolver (used `entry.defaultTemplateFile`) instead of calling **`getDefaultTemplateBytes`**, leaving that one
store export with no production importer. The other three store exports are reachable via the route
(`saveDefaultTemplate`, `getDefaultTemplate`, `clearDefaultTemplate`).

**Implementer action:** wire `getDefaultTemplateBytes` to its production consumer (the Step-3 client resolver
`resolveDefaultTemplateFile`) IN THE SAME WAVE, or the sweep reds and the ledger constant (368) breaks. This is
not a defect in the test files (none of my tests touch the sweep); it is the in-wave-importer obligation the
brief asked me to re-derive.
