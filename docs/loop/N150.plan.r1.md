### Re-triage corrections the TDD seat must encode

- **C1 (load-bearing) — the Row-1-emission guard must cover FOUR non-embedded spread sites, not two.**
  The design (§0, §4.5, §7 T-F3b) names the non-embedded spreads as `route.js:796` and `:914` only.
  Live HEAD spreads `...projectExampleField` at **four** non-embedded sites: the streaming producer
  `streamAnswer` at **`:323`** (answer mode) and **`:335`** (points mode) — reached only on the
  non-embedded path by the `body?.stream === true && !wantsEmbedded(body?.engine)` gate at `:631` —
  **plus** the non-streaming Gemini branches at `:796` (answer) and `:914` (points). The embedded
  branches are `:664` (answer, `embeddedRoleTermsFlag` at `:712`, no `projectExampleField`) and
  `:812` (points, `embeddedRoleTermsFlag` at `:838`, no `projectExampleField`). **The robust,
  rename-proof invariant is the co-occurrence:** every payload that spreads `geminiRoleTermsFlag`
  (streaming `roleTermsFlag` computed via `geminiRoleTermsFlag` at `:302`; non-streaming `:787`,
  `:912`) also spreads `...projectExampleField`, and every payload that spreads
  `embeddedRoleTermsFlag` (`:712`, `:838`) spreads none. T-F3b must assert this co-occurrence (or
  enumerate all four + both), **not** only `:796`/`:914`, or a future edit to the streaming path
  silently breaks tech-terms enablement while the guard stays green.

- **C2 — the "one shared gate" (F3) cannot be byte-identical across the two fires; the fetch-probe
  differs.** `liveFetchAvailable()` (`useDraftAnswer.js:51`) is a namespace probe for the
  **`fetchProjectExampleLive`** export. Tech-terms needs its own probe for **`fetchTechTerms`**
  (same Vitest stale-mock reason, §4.2 below). So the genuinely shared predicate is the **Row-1
  enablement core** `grounding.applicationId && example && <token current>`; each fire then ANDs its
  own export probe. T-F3a must assert the **core** is shared (both fires gate on `applicationId`,
  the same `example`, and the token), and must **not** force `fireTechTerms` to probe
  `fetchProjectExampleLive` — that would wire the wrong export.

- **C3 — `findClaimVerbWordIndex` is module-PRIVATE.** `questionVocabulary.js:343` declares it as a
  non-exported `function`. The design permits reusing it "if it proves a clean fit," but reuse would
  require **exporting** it — widening `questionVocabulary.js`'s surface and churning the TR-1 census.
  **Plan decision: do NOT reuse it.** `techTermDetailHonesty.js` uses the self-contained
  `FIRST_PERSON_CLAIM_RES` list (design §3.5, the primary), which keeps the detector's contract
  pinned by its own canary + no-op controls and avoids a cross-module coupling and a census move.

**RED-on-HEAD canary (re-confirmed):** Grep `techTerms|tech-terms|techTerm|TechTerm|TECH_TERMS`
over `hello-world/` → 0. Positive canary: `projectExampleLive` returns the Row-2 module + its three
producers. N150 is wholly unimplemented; every structural criterion is RED, not vacuously green.

## 2. Decisions I resolved (routed to the plan)

- **OWNER DECISION 1 — detail-route metering → BOUNDED 40/10min (adopt design's recommendation (a)).**
  The per-buzzword detail endpoint is the repeatable per-click model call — the exact unbounded-loop
  shape `/expand`'s limiter guards (`expand/route.js:48`, `adoption.test.js:45-49`). I adopt a
  module-scope `techTermDetailLimiter = createRateLimiter({ limit: 40, windowMs: 600_000, prefix:
  "copilot-tech-term-detail" })`, declared before `POST`, denied-still-increments, 429 built from
  `rateLimitHeaders(decision)`. This gives `adoption.test.js` its required **one BOUNDED** entry.
  The **generation** route is the post-answer one-shot (one call per drafted question, like
  project-example) → **DEFERRED/unmetered**, the required **one DEFERRED** entry. **Owner may flip
  the detail route to DEFERRED;** if so, the BOUNDED entry + the `expand`-shaped describe block move
  to a DEFERRED entry and the detail route drops its limiter. One of the two MUST land either way.

- **OWNER DECISION 2 — `TECH_TERMS_COUNT` default 6 (named, owner-tunable).** Kept as an exported
  const in `techTermsGen.js` (satisfies the RETRO-R3/N137 standing rule for a seat-flagged tunable).
  Its retune route is the N135 owner probe, not a future reactive chunk.

- **F3 mechanism (per C2 above):** in each of the three producers, extract the Row-1 core into one
  named local the two fires share. In `useDraftAnswer` (where `grounding`/`id`/`token` are in scope):
  `const exampleRowEnabled = (example) => !!grounding.applicationId && !!example && latestTokenByIdRef.current.get(id) === token;`
  then `fireProjectExampleLive` guards `exampleRowEnabled(example) && liveFetchAvailable()` and
  `fireTechTerms` guards `exampleRowEnabled(example) && techTermsFetchAvailable()`. In
  `useSampleAnswer`/`useRoomQuestions` the core is `!!appId && !!example` (their existing shape) with
  each fire's own probe. This is the design's "one named local, both fires call it," made precise.
