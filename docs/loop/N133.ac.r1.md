# Acceptance criteria - backlog N133 (truthy-but-contentless map slot defeats opts.entry in openResumePreview)

Seat: AC + RED test (1 + 4b, narrow). Round: r1. Author artifacts: this file and the RED tests appended to
`hello-world/app/hooks/useDocumentPreview.openEntry.test.js`. No production file was touched.

Path note: the hook is `hello-world/app/hooks/useDocumentPreview.js` (the brief said `lib/copilot/`; it is not
there). This AC sits in the repo-root `docs/loop/`, beside `docs/backlog.yml` and the N132/N134 artifacts.

## 0. The defect, grounded against HEAD (read 2026-10-07)

`openResumePreview(job, opts)` in `useDocumentPreview.js` reads ONE object `t`:

    const t = tailoringMap[job.id] || opts.entry || {};

and uses `t` for three things: the tab (`wantsCover = opts.tab === "cover" && previewScopeAvailable(t, "cover")`),
the research warm-up (`if (previewScopeAvailable(t, "cover")) startBackgroundResearch({jobId, company, jobTitle:
t.generatedJobTitle || job.title, posting: t.jobDescription || job.description})`), and the header
(`title: t.generatedJobTitle || job.title`, `posting: t.jobDescription || job.description`).

`||` falls through only on a FALSY slot. A slot like `{status:"error", error:"Upload a resume first."}` (written by
`handleTailorJob` in `app/page.js`, whose first guard is `updateTailoringJob(job.id, {status:"error", error:"Upload a
resume first."})`) is truthy, so it wins, and all three decisions are made from an object with no resume and no cover.

`openApplicationPreview` in `app/page.js` is correct: `keepExisting = !!(existing?.result || existing?.status ===
"tailoring")` is false for such a slot, so it overwrites the slot via `setTailoringMap` (uncommitted when the hook
runs, same tick), computes `tab` from the rebuilt `openEntry`, and passes `{tab, entry: openEntry}`. The hook ignores it.

Reachability (honest): narrow, as the backlog says. The rehydration `useEffect` in `app/page.js` repairs slots only for
jobs in `trackedJobs`, and `updateTailoringJob` MERGES (so a failed re-run on a slot that already had a cover letter
keeps the letter). The bug needs a slot created by a failure with no prior content, for a row not yet repaired (click
before the effect, or a row whose job is not in `trackedJobs`). Not verified in a browser; severity stays minor.

## 0b. Two corrections to the backlog entry

1. **The defect is not only cover-only rows.** A resume+cover application with the same stale slot opens on the correct
   'resume' tab (handler says so) but the research warm-up is SKIPPED too, because the warm-up gate also reads the stale
   `t`. Measured: the RED test "resume+cover entry + tab:'resume' => STILL warms research once" fails on HEAD.
2. **"Honor the handler's already-computed tab" (backlog option B) is not sufficient alone.** It repairs the tab and
   nothing else. Measured on a scratch tree: variant M-B (tab honours `opts.entry`, `t` left stale) turns the 5 tab
   REDs green and leaves 13 of 18 RED (warm-up and header). The fix must change which object `t` is.

## 1. Acceptance criteria

### AC-N133-1 - the precedence rule (the exact rule the fix must satisfy)

Let S = `tailoringMap[job.id]` as read at render time, E = `opts.entry`. Call S **contentless** when S is truthy AND
`previewScopeAvailable(S, "resume")` is false AND `previewScopeAvailable(S, "cover")` is false.

- (a) S falsy: `t` = E, else `{}`. Unchanged.
- (b) S contentless and E supplied: the open behaves **exactly as if S were absent**. E is the single source for the
  tab decision, the warm-up gate and args, and the header title/posting. No split reads (cover availability from E but
  title from S is a defect).
- (c) S previewable in cover: S wins over E (live-wins), including title, posting and warm-up args, even when E also has
  a cover letter. Unchanged from the N132 fix.
- (d) E not supplied (every caller except `openApplicationPreview`): unchanged, whatever `opts.tab` says.
- Unspecified, deliberately: S has a resume but no cover while E has a cover. Unreachable through the only `opts.entry`
  caller (`openApplicationPreview` passes `existing` itself whenever `existing.result` is set). Either "S has content in
  any scope" or "S has the needed scope" satisfies this AC; the tests do not pin it (both reference variants pass).
- Stale shapes covered (each asserted truthy and previewable in neither scope): `{}`, `{status:"error"}`,
  `{status:"error", error}`, `{status:"done", result:"", resultLines:[], coverLetterResultLines:[]}`. `status:"tailoring"`
  is excluded: the handler passes E === S in that case, so there is nothing to disagree about.

### AC-N133-2 - cover-only row, stale slot: correct tab, research warm, entry header

With a contentless S and a cover-only E and `opts.tab: "cover"`: `resumePreview.tab === "cover"`; `startBackgroundResearch`
is called exactly once with `{jobId, company, jobTitle, posting}` taken from E; `resumePreview.title` and
`resumePreview.posting` come from E. Also through the handler-shaped sequence (write the map and open in ONE act, with
`api.openResumePreview` the pre-write closure): tab `cover`, one warm-up.

### AC-N133-3 - resume+cover row, stale slot: the handler's tab stands, research still warms

With a contentless S and a resume+cover E and `opts.tab: "resume"`: `resumePreview.tab === "resume"` (not forced to
cover) AND `startBackgroundResearch` is called exactly once with E's title/posting.

### AC-N133-4 - nothing else moves

(i) Contentless S and no E: tab `resume`, research cold, even with `opts.tab: "cover"`. (ii) Contentless S and a
contentless E: tab `resume`, research cold (no content invented). (iii) Live-wins per AC-N133-1(c). (iv) The 8 existing
tests in `useDocumentPreview.openEntry.test.js` and all of `app/applicationPreviewOpenPath.test.js` stay green. (v) The
fix is confined to `openResumePreview` in `useDocumentPreview.js`: no `page.js` change is needed, and no new export.

## 2. The RED test and why it cannot pass vacuously

File: `hello-world/app/hooks/useDocumentPreview.openEntry.test.js` (the file that already drives the real
`openResumePreview` through the real hook). Harness change: `Probe` now also records `latestMap` and `setMapRef` (needed
for the same-tick test); nothing existing was edited or weakened.

**31 new tests: 18 RED on HEAD, 13 green controls/guards.** Rows that could fail: 31.

Real-tree run on HEAD, this file only: `Tests  18 failed | 21 passed (39)` (8 pre-existing + 31 new).

RED (18) = per stale shape (x4): "cover-only entry + tab:'cover' => opens on the 'cover' tab"; "cover-only entry => warms
company research once, from the entry's title and posting"; "cover-only entry => the preview header (title, posting) is
read from the entry, not the stale slot"; "resume+cover entry + tab:'resume' => STILL warms research once (a cover letter
exists)" (16), plus the handler-shaped "opens on the 'cover' tab" and "warms company research once" (2). Observed failure
reasons: `expected 'resume' to be 'cover'`; `expected "vi.fn()" to be called 1 times, but got 0 times`; `expected 'Staff
Engineer' to be 'Staff Engineer (Tailored)'` - the stale-slot reads, not an exception or a missing import.

Green on HEAD, must stay green (13): per shape "[precondition] the slot is truthy yet previewable in NEITHER scope" and
"[guard] resume+cover entry + tab:'resume' => stays on 'resume'" (8); "[positive control] the SAME cover-only entry with the
slot ABSENT => ..." (the stale slot is the only variable); "[guard] contentless slot and NO opts.entry ..."; "[guard]
contentless slot AND contentless opts.entry => no content is invented"; "[guard] a LIVE slot that has a cover letter beats
a different opts.entry that also has one (live-wins)"; "the write itself committed (so only the closure read was stale)".
