# N92 - Acceptance criteria, round 1

Feature: three controls for positioning and smoothing inserted company facts in the cover letter.
Owner request, verbatim (two parts, same day 2026-09-28):

1. "the fact insertions are working now, but now i need controls to move each fact forward or backward
   a sentence in the letter, and also a control to use the llm to make the transition into the fact
   sentence and the sentence after the fact sentence more natural"
2. "also make it to where i can tell the app to apply the positioning of facts forward on all future
   generated cover letters"

- **CONTROL A** (deterministic): move one inserted fact one SENTENCE forward / backward.
- **CONTROL B** (LLM): rewrite the fact sentence and its neighbours so the transition reads naturally.
- **CONTROL C** (persisted preference): a per-user toggle that applies a standing FORWARD positioning
  to facts on all FUTURE generated letters (Control A's forward-move applied automatically at insert time).

They are ONE feature about fact positioning and must stay coherent. A and C share one positioning primitive.

Author: AC seat (Opus). This artifact binds design (1b/1c/1d/1e), the plan, the 4b tests, the
implementer and the verifier. It is NOT a design and NOT tests: where it names a mechanism it does so
only to make a criterion checkable, and design may choose a different mechanism that satisfies the same
criterion.

## 1. Decisions I am making (stated so downstream does not re-litigate)

- **D1 - "Sentence" and move unit.** Control A moves the fact's recorded text span among the
  inter-sentence SLOTS of the letter body. Sentence boundaries are computed deterministically over the
  body paragraphs (design owns the exact segmenter; it must be a single pure function). One click = one
  slot. This is a genuine NEW rule (a new invention per the traps-spec "revised facts hold, new
  inventions fail" note) - attack it hardest in check.
- **D2 - Boundary behaviour.** A move CROSSES paragraph boundaries within the letter BODY (the owner said
  "in the letter", not "in the paragraph"), because a paragraph-only move is a permanent no-op for a fact
  in a one-sentence paragraph. The movable range is bounded to body paragraphs: a move may never place a
  fact into the greeting (line 0) or into a closing/signature block, and never outside the region
  `markInsertedFacts` can highlight. What would change this ruling: an owner/UX preference for
  paragraph-bounded moves; if so, edges AC-A6/A7 still apply with the range narrowed.
- **D3 - Control C is an independent boolean modifier, not a placement id.** Placement decides WHERE a
  fact starts; the forward toggle applies exactly one Control-A forward-move AFTER placement resolves.
  Rationale: the two axes are orthogonal; folding "forward" into the placement id list conflates them and
  multiplies options. Deliberately a boolean (one nudge), not an integer offset - the owner said
  "forward", one direction. An offset is a future extension, out of scope now.
- **D4 - Control B on embedded: disable with an honest reason (AC default).** No silent no-op, no
  deterministic pretend-smoothing. FLAGGED to the owner as a reversible product call (fallback vs
  disable). See AC-B9 and Section 6.
- **D5 - Control C load-failure and not-yet-loaded both resolve to "do not apply forward"** (the safe
  direction: never fabricate a positioning the user did not set). See AC-C6.
- **D6 - Control C applies to the automatic insert path only** (autoInsertFactsForJob), not to a manual
  per-article placement the user set by hand in the research dialog (an explicit manual choice outranks a
  standing default). See AC-C8.

## 2. CONTROL A - move a fact forward / backward one sentence

**RED-on-HEAD basis for the whole group:** no move control, handler, or move primitive exists at HEAD
(grep `moveFact|moveInsertedFact|moveForward|moveBackward` in production = none, 2026-09-28). Every AC
below fails on HEAD because the thing it asserts does not exist yet. Where a criterion is instead a
non-regression guard, it is marked GUARD.

- **AC-A1 (the primitive exists and is pure).** A single pure function computes, for a given letter
  (`lines: string[]`) and a located fact record `{id,text,lineIndex,offset}` and a direction
  (`forward`|`backward`), the new `{lines, record}` with the fact's text relocated by exactly one
  sentence slot, or an unchanged result when the move is a no-op (fact already at the boundary slot).
  It never mutates its input and returns the same `lines` reference on a no-op (the invariant
  `planCoverFacts`/`planRemoveFact` already hold).
  *Instrument:* a pure unit test of the primitive (vitest, no jsdom), like `factInsertion.js`'s own tests.
  *RED on HEAD:* function does not exist.
- **AC-A2 (one click = one sentence).** Clicking forward moves the fact past exactly one sentence
  boundary; backward moves it before exactly one. Two forward clicks move it two slots. Direction is not
  swapped (a forward control must move toward the END of the letter).
  *Instrument:* jsdom render of the strip + click, asserting the rendered/stored order of sentences.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a swapped or off-by-one move is a defect; assert the exact
  resulting sentence order, not merely "position changed".
- **AC-A3 (crosses paragraph boundaries, bounded to body).** A fact at the last sentence of a body
  paragraph, moved forward, lands at the first slot of the NEXT body paragraph. A fact at the first
  sentence of the first body paragraph, moved backward, is a no-op (AC-A6). A move never places the fact
  in line 0 (greeting) or in a closing/signature block.
  *Instrument:* pure unit test with a multi-paragraph fixture including a greeting.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a move into the greeting/closing is the unsafe failure; the passing
  case is the bounded one.
- **AC-A4 (reversible, exactly).** For any fact not at a boundary, forward-then-backward returns the
  letter to byte-identical `lines` AND the record to the identical `{lineIndex, offset}`. Backward-then-
  forward likewise.
  *Instrument:* pure unit test (round-trip).
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a move that "mostly" reverses (whitespace drift, offset drift) is a
  defect; assert byte-identity.
- **AC-A5 (record stays consistent with the letter - N81/N90 class).** After ANY single move, every
  surviving record in `entry.insertedFacts` locates its OWN text at its stored `offset` in its stored
  `lineIndex` (the exact invariant `relocateSurvivors` guards for removal, `useCompanyResearch.js:615-627`),
  and the moved fact appears in the letter EXACTLY ONCE (no duplication).
  *Instrument:* the class-invariant test style of `removeInsertedFact.coalesced.rc.test.js` (assert the
  invariant over all survivors, not a fixture-specific position).
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a stale offset that makes a fact unremovable/unhighlightable, or a
  duplicated fact, is the exact N61/N81 defect class - the passing case is one occurrence + all offsets valid.
- **AC-A6 (boundary no-op is honest).** A fact already at the first movable slot has its backward control
  DISABLED (not a silent click that does nothing); a fact at the last movable slot has forward DISABLED.
  A disabled control is announced to assistive tech (aria-disabled or disabled attribute), not merely
  greyed.
  *Instrument:* jsdom render asserting the disabled attribute/aria at the boundary and enabled off it.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a live-looking control that no-ops silently is the defect; the safe
  state is visibly/announced disabled.
- **AC-A7 (two adjacent facts).** With two facts on the same line (coalesced) or adjacent across a
  boundary, moving one PAST the other leaves BOTH records locating their own text (AC-A5), the two facts
  in the new relative order, and neither excised or merged.
  *Instrument:* pure + jsdom test with two same-line facts.
  *RED on HEAD.*
- **AC-A8 (hand-edited letter: safe no-op, never a guess).** If the letter was hand-edited so the fact's
  recorded span is no longer at `(lineIndex, offset)`, a move is a no-op that leaves `lines` unchanged and
  surfaces an honest reason (mirroring `planRemoveFact`'s stale-locator no-op, `factInsertion.js:240-241`),
  never a relocation guessed against a different occurrence.
  *Instrument:* unit test with a stale locator.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* guessing a different occurrence and moving the wrong text is the
  unsafe failure; the safe outcome is the no-op.
- **AC-A9 (not gated on cover bytes).** A move proceeds on the letter TEXT even when there are no engine
  cover bytes to splice (restored chip / version switch), exactly as `removeInsertedFact` proceeds
  (`useCompanyResearch.js:638-646`); when a splice cannot apply, stored bytes are dropped to `""` so the
  download rebuilds from the moved lines, never shipping stale bytes carrying the old position.
  *Instrument:* hook rc test with `hasCoverBytes=false` and a splice-refusal fixture.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* trapping the fact at its old position (unremovable/unmovable) or
  shipping stale bytes is the defect.

## 3. CONTROL B - LLM-smooth the transition

**RED-on-HEAD basis:** no smoothing control, handler, engine method, or route exists at HEAD (grep
`smoothTransition|smoothFact` = none). Criteria fail on HEAD by absence unless marked GUARD.

- **AC-B1 (scope of the rewrite is pinned to exactly three sentences).** Smoothing may change ONLY: the
  sentence BEFORE the fact (the "transition into"), the fact sentence, and the sentence AFTER it. After a
  smoothing, the letter differs from before ONLY within the contiguous span covering those sentences;
  every other paragraph and sentence is byte-identical.
  *Instrument:* diff the pre/post `lines` in a unit/hook test; assert equality outside the 3-sentence span.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a rewrite that touches the whole paragraph or a distant sentence is
  the defect; the passing case is the bounded diff. This is also the primary blast-radius bound on hallucination.
- **AC-B2 (scope shrinks at a boundary, never crosses into an adjacent paragraph).** If the fact is the
  first sentence of its paragraph, there is no "before" to touch and smoothing does not reach into the
  previous paragraph; if it is the last, no "after" and it does not reach the next. The greeting and any
  closing block are never touched.
  *Instrument:* unit test with the fact at each paragraph edge.
  *RED on HEAD.*
- **AC-B3 (fact substance preserved - FAITHFULNESS, the dominant risk).** The smoothed fact sentence must
  still assert the same fact and must not introduce a new verifiable claim (a number, date, name, or
  outcome) that was not in the fact's own recorded text or the two neighbour sentences before smoothing.
  *Instrument (checkable proxy):* (a) a wire-level assertion that the engine request carries ONLY the
  three in-scope sentences plus the fact's recorded text as the material it may use, and a system
  instruction forbidding new claims (see AC-B7); (b) AC-B1's diff bound; (c) a rejection guard AC-B5.
  *Instrument (NOT fully falsifiable by a unit test):* "the model did not invent a subtle claim" is a
  semantic property no deterministic test fully decides. This criterion is CARRIED by the proxies above
  plus a HELD-OUT manual review the verifier runs on real engine output (a real gemini call, several
  facts), reported as such. **I am naming this as the one criterion that cannot be made fully
  falsifiable in an automated test** - the automated part is the proxies; the residual is a mandated
  manual/held-out check. *Failure direction:* an added or altered claim reaching the candidate's voice is
  the worst outcome this app can produce; when in doubt the guard must REJECT (AC-B5), not show.
- **AC-B4 (provenance preserved).** Smoothing never changes the fact record's `url`/`title`/`id`; after a
  smoothing the source link (when `safeExternalHref` accepts the url) still renders for that fact and the
  fact is still one-click removable, with its span re-located to the smoothed text (AC-A5 invariant).
  *Instrument:* jsdom render + hook rc test asserting url/title/id unchanged and remove still excises cleanly.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a smoothed clause that can no longer be removed or has lost its
  source is the unsafe failure.
- **AC-B5 (bad rewrite is REJECTED to a no-op, never shown).** If the engine errors, times out, returns
  empty, or returns output that fails the scope guard (changes text outside the 3-sentence span) or the
  faithfulness proxy, the smoothing is rejected: the letter `lines` are left byte-identical to
  pre-smoothing, nothing is persisted, an honest error is surfaced, and the decision is logged with a
  negative outcome (AC-X1).
  *Instrument:* hook rc tests with a stubbed engine returning error / empty / out-of-scope output; assert
  `lines` unchanged and no PUT to `/api/accepted-facts`.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* showing/persisting an unverified rewrite is the defect; the safe
  outcome is the pre-smoothing text preserved.
- **AC-B6 (reversible / undo).** The pre-smoothing text of the three in-scope sentences is captured before
  the call; the user can restore it with one click, returning the letter to byte-identical pre-smoothing
  `lines` and re-locating the fact. Undo persists like any other edit (survives repaint + download).
  *Instrument:* jsdom render + click smooth then undo; assert byte-identity and persistence.
  *RED on HEAD.*
- **AC-B7 (egress is scoped - only the in-scope material leaves).** The smoothing engine request payload
  contains ONLY the three in-scope sentences and the fact's recorded text (and whatever framing the
  contract needs), and does NOT send the whole letter, the resume, the job description, or unrelated
  paragraphs.
  *Instrument:* drive the real handler with `fetch`/engine spied, assert on actual payload contents, WITH
  a positive control that records a real request on that path (the egress-trap rule from loop-traps-spec).
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* over-sending is a privacy defect; the passing case is the minimal payload.
- **AC-B8 (strictly user-initiated - spend).** The smoothing engine call fires ONLY on an explicit user
  click. It never fires on: the N72 auto-insert path, generation, a preview repaint, a reload, a version
  switch, Control A, or Control C. STRUCTURAL form preferred: `autoInsertFactsForJob` and the generation
  path do not import/reference the smoothing function at all (make the wrong thing impossible, not merely
  forbidden). No numeric cap (N65 salary precedent: user-initiated is the control).
  *Instrument:* (a) a static/structural test that the unattended path does not reference the smoothing
  seam; (b) engine-spy tests over auto-insert/repaint/reload asserting zero smoothing calls, each with a
  positive control that a click DOES call it.
  *RED on HEAD (partly GUARD):* today no smoothing seam exists, so "auto path does not call it" is
  vacuously true; the guard becomes load-bearing once B exists - state this honestly. The click-fires-a-call
  half is genuinely RED (no control exists).
- **AC-B9 (engine dependence).** On the embedded engine (`wantsEmbedded` true), Control B is DISABLED with
  an honest, visible reason ("needs an AI engine" or similar) and fires NO engine call. On gemini/external
  it is enabled. The disabled state is announced to assistive tech.
  *Instrument:* jsdom render under each engine selection; assert enabled/disabled + no call under embedded.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a control that appears usable under embedded but silently does
  nothing is the defect; the safe state is disabled-with-reason. (See Section 6 - owner call.)

## 4. CONTROL C - persisted "position facts forward on future letters"

**RED-on-HEAD basis:** no such preference key, toggle, or insert-time modifier exists at HEAD (the
user-prefs sanitize has no forward key; `autoInsertFactsForJob` applies placement only). Criteria fail on
HEAD by absence unless marked GUARD.

- **AC-C1 (composition with placement is pinned).** The forward preference is an INDEPENDENT boolean
  modifier applied AFTER placement resolves: at insert time the fact is first placed per the placement
  pref (`defaultPlacement || DEFAULT_PLACEMENT`), then if the forward toggle is ON, exactly one
  Control-A forward-move is applied to the placed fact, subject to the same body-boundary rule (AC-A3);
  if that move would exceed the last movable slot it is a no-op (no error). With the toggle OFF the placed
  position is unchanged from today.
  *Instrument:* pure unit test over the insert planner with the toggle on/off crossed with each placement id.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* the toggle must not silently change WHICH placement is used, only
  apply the nudge on top.
- **AC-C2 (shares Control A's primitive).** The forward nudge Control C applies is the SAME pure function
  Control A uses (AC-A1) - not a second, divergent implementation.
  *Instrument:* the code path is the same symbol (structural/import test) and the two produce identical
  results on a shared fixture.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a divergent copy is the defect (two positioning rules that drift).
- **AC-C3 (persistence rides user-prefs additively; guards not weakened).** The preference is a new
  allowlisted key on `/api/user-prefs` sanitized as a strict boolean (absent/non-boolean dropped), added
  the same way `ALLOWED_BOOLEAN_KEYS` entries are (`route.js:9-13,63-64`). The existing sanitize behaviour
  for `coverFactPlacement`, `appliedSort`, `excludedTitleKeywords` and the boolean keys is byte-unchanged.
  *Instrument:* route unit test: the new key round-trips only as a boolean; a garbage value is dropped;
  and the existing sanitize tests still pass (non-regression).
  *RED on HEAD (definition) + GUARD (existing sanitize).* *Failure direction:* a type-only or absent-guard
  that lets garbage through is the defect (the N62/N82 sanitize discipline).
- **AC-C4 (last hop: set -> generate -> forward, visible AND downloaded).** With the toggle ON, a newly
  generated letter that auto-inserts facts has those facts positioned one forward-slot from their
  placement, visible in the preview highlight AND present at that position in the downloaded document.
  *Instrument:* hook/integration test through `autoInsertFactsForJob` + the preview render + the download
  rebuild path (the last-hop the repo repeatedly drops behind a green suite).
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a preference that changes stored state but not the visible/downloaded
  letter is the exact last-hop defect class this repo keeps shipping.
- **AC-C5 (turn-off-able / not one-way - N82's lesson).** The toggle is bidirectional: setting it to false
  is settable, persists, and future letters place facts WITHOUT the forward nudge. `false` is not
  swallowed as "no preference".
  *Instrument:* route + hook test toggling true->false and asserting the OFF behaviour at insert time.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* a write-once/never-clears preference is the N82 defect.
- **AC-C6 (failed / not-yet-loaded load resolves SAFE - N86, do not step on the rake).** A failed GET, a
  non-ok GET, or a not-yet-loaded state must NOT be read as "forward is on": at insert time the modifier
  is applied only on an explicit loaded `true`. Not-yet-loaded and load-failure both behave as "do not
  apply forward". Control C must not add a SECOND independent fetch race; it reuses a shared preference
  source (the same one N86's fix consolidates), so the settings UI and the insert path cannot silently
  disagree.
  *Instrument:* hook test simulating a failed/pending load asserting the insert path does not apply the
  nudge; a test that both consumers read one source.
  *RED on HEAD.* *Failure direction:* fabricating a forward the user did not set (moving facts they did
  not ask to move) is the unsafe failure; the safe default is no-nudge. Cross-ref N86 (still open); this
  criterion depends on N86's shared-source direction - if N86 is unresolved when this lands, design must
  say how Control C avoids adding a new race rather than inheriting the fix.
- **AC-C7 (not retroactive).** Turning the preference on/off changes only what a FUTURE insertion resolves
  to; it never re-plans or moves facts in an existing `tailoringMap` entry or a stored letter version
  (mirroring `useCoverFactPlacement`'s AC-A4 note at `:52-57`).
  *Instrument:* test that toggling the pref does not mutate an existing entry's `lines`/`insertedFacts`.
  *RED on HEAD (definition) / GUARD (no-mutation).*
- **AC-C8 (scope: automatic insert path only; cron out of scope; manual choice outranks).** Control C
  applies to the client automatic insert path (`autoInsertFactsForJob`). It does NOT apply to a placement
  the user set by hand per-article in the research dialog (explicit manual choice wins). It has NO effect
  on the auto-tailor cron, which inserts no facts today (Section 0 finding) - this is a stated scope
  boundary, not a gap to fill in this chunk (cross-ref N61/N73). If server-side generation later inserts
  facts, honouring the preference there is a follow-up.
  *Instrument:* test that the manual research-dialog placement is unaffected; documentation of the cron
  boundary (no test can assert a negative-scope on code that does not exist - stated as a boundary).
  *RED on HEAD (manual-outranks) / boundary (cron).*

## 5. Cross-cutting

- **AC-X1 (decision logging - both/all controls).** Each of Control A (move), Control B (smooth) and
  Control C (a change to the forward pref that affects an insertion) records its outcome through
  `recordDecision` so the downloaded activity log states what happened, with outcomes drawn from the
  closed vocabulary INCLUDING a negative (a move no-op / a rejected smoothing / a skipped nudge), and a
  closed field vocabulary carrying NO letter text, company, url, or title (N77) - counts/enums only
  (e.g. a direction enum, a reason code, an outcome). `decisionCoverage.sweep.test.js` stays green in
  BOTH directions (every recording module is listed; every listed module records).
  *Instrument:* `lib/activityLog/decisionCoverage.sweep.test.js` (existing) + a per-control recording test.
  *RED on HEAD:* today these controls record nothing (removal itself is not on the ledger); adding a
  `recordDecision(` call turns the sweep RED until the ledger declares the module, which is the intended
  forcing function.
  *STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINT for design (1b), not a value I fix:* the ledger is one entry PER MODULE and
  `DocumentPreviewMount.js` already owns `fact-auto-insert`. Design must choose one of: (a) record the new
  controls from a different module each with its own ledger entry (e.g. the useCompanyResearch handlers);
  (b) reuse the `fact-auto-insert` id with an expanded field/outcome vocabulary and a discriminator field;
  or (c) change the ledger to key by (module,id). The AC does not pick; it requires the sweep stay green
  and the field vocabulary stay closed and text-free. *Failure direction:* leaking any letter/company/url
  text into the shared, downloaded log is the unsafe failure.
- **AC-X2 (minimize-clicks + cumulative surface - 6b).** The controls are inline per-fact controls next to
  the existing Remove control - no menu, no wizard, no mode switch, sensible defaults, one click each.
  Each control has a distinct accessible name (no Tooltip wrapper stealing it - `InsertedFactsStrip.js:20-22`),
  is keyboard reachable, and disabled states carry an honest reason.
  *Instrument:* jsdom render asserting per-control aria-label + keyboard reachability + click count.
  *RED on HEAD.*
  **COMPOSITE-SURFACE COST (flagged for the 8b re-judge, per rule 6b):** this chunk takes each fact ROW
  in `InsertedFactsStrip` from ~2 controls (source link + Remove) to as many as ~5 (source, back, forward,
  smooth, Remove), plus a panel-level forward toggle for Control C. No per-diff gate measures a surface's
  cumulative legibility; the 8b UX re-judge must weigh the row's total control density and may require
  grouping/overflow. I am not redesigning the surface here - only making the composite cost visible at
  specification time.
- **AC-X3 (persistence + repaint + download for A and B).** A move (A) and a smoothing (B), like removal,

## 6. Escalation to the owner (one reversible product call)

**Control B on the embedded engine.** The embedded engine has no LLM, so "use the llm to make it natural"
cannot run there. AC default (D4/AC-B9): DISABLE Control B with an honest reason on embedded. The
alternative would be a deterministic non-LLM "tidy" fallback, which cannot deliver the owner's intent and
risks looking like it did nothing. This is a genuine product call the owner can reverse in one sentence;
I have decided the safe default and surfaced the alternative rather than blocking on it. No other item
here needs an owner decision.

## 7. Out of scope (explicit, so design does not widen and the verifier does not file it as missing)

- The hiring email and the resume: facts live in the cover letter only (`factInsertion.js` plans the cover
  letter only; the hiring email is plain text - `factInsertion.js:346-349`). Both controls act on
  cover-letter facts.
- Drag-and-drop or arbitrary repositioning: the owner asked for forward/backward by one sentence (discrete
  steps) and a forward default - not free placement.
- Re-researching or changing WHICH fact/source is inserted: Control B smooths wording; it never swaps the
  fact or its provenance.
- Control C on the auto-tailor cron / any server-side generation: the cron inserts no facts today
  (Section 0). Out of scope until server-side insertion exists (N61/N73).
- An integer forward OFFSET preference (N-sentences): Control C is a boolean (one nudge). A magnitude is a
  future extension.
- A numeric spend cap on Control B: strictly user-initiated is the control (N65 precedent).
- N86's shared-source/loading-state fix itself: Control C must not reintroduce the hazard and should reuse
  the shared source, but fixing N86 is its own open item.
