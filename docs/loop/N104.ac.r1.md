# Acceptance criteria — N104: weakness summary (generated doc vs posting) + truthful "regenerate to address these weaknesses" in the preview/edit modal — r1

Seat: AC (1). Round: r1. Artifact only. No source edited, no git writes.

**What N104 is.** Two coupled controls in the preview/edit modal: **(a) a WEAKNESS SUMMARY** comparing a generated résumé/cover letter to the job posting — missing/under-weighted requirements, keyword gaps, vague/unsupported claims, and the requirements wording alone cannot resolve — and **(b) a "REGENERATE to address these weaknesses" action** that feeds the summary back into generation. N104 is **largely a CONSUMER** of the shipped N103 review chokepoint and N106 reviewer; the **novel and safety-critical** part is the **truthful regenerate**.

**The one-line safety thesis (why this seat is careful).** A "fill the weaknesses" button is, by default, a fabrication engine: the straightest way to "address" a missing requirement is to invent experience that satisfies it. The whole value of doing this in the app rather than a chat LLM is that the regenerate **closes gaps truthfully or leaves them open and says so** — never by fabricating. The repo already learned this concretely: the modal currently **disables** its re-tailor controls on an Ideal job precisely because the standard route "would replace the gated Application-ready text with an ungated one" (F-C7). N104 re-introduces a regenerate; it must re-introduce it through the gate, not around it.

Binding sources read **first-hand this round**: `docs/loop/N103.design.r1.md` (the shipped chokepoint design), `docs/loop/N106.ac.r2.md` (the reviewer contract + coverage), `docs/loop/N105.ac.r2.md` (the truthfulness-gate ACs N104 inherits), `hello-world/lib/review/runDocumentReview.js`, `hello-world/lib/review/reviewPresentation.js`, `hello-world/app/components/preview/DocumentReviewSection.js`, `loop-traps-spec`. Facts F-C1..F-C9 were established by a code-mapping sweep this round and are marked **CITED (sweep)**; a downstream checker must re-grep before pinning any line number (`loop-traps-search`: renames/edits move them). ## 2. Acceptance criteria

### — HIGHEST-SEVERITY (SAFETY) ACs —

### AC-1 (SAFETY — THE no-fabrication criterion) — a regenerate NEVER introduces an unsupported claim to close a gap; its submittable output passes the SAME truthfulness gate as N105, and it routes through the gated path, never the ungated standard route
Every claim in the regenerated **application-ready/submittable** document (each bullet-sized span, app-minted ids) must, when run through the N105 gate `applicationReadyGate({candidateSpans, realMaterial})` against the user's real material, be **`kept`** (supported) — OR be surfaced only under the explicit `UNVERIFIED_FLAG`, OR be dropped/left-open. A gap is closed **TRUTHFULLY** (surfacing genuinely-supported content the first pass under-weighted) or **LEFT OPEN** and reported as genuinely-unresolvable (AC-2). **A regenerate can never add a claim the gate would `drop` and present it as a plain truthful bullet.** The regenerate output is gate-cleaned **downstream of whatever the engine produced** (F-C4), and it **re-enters the gated path (`runIdealPipeline`/`applicationReadyGate`), never the standard ungated `/api/tailor` route** (the F-C7 hazard).
- **Why "impossible" not "forbidden" (rule 4):** the submittable text the user sees is the gate's `kept`+`flagged` output, not the engine's raw output — a fabricated span has no route to a plain bullet because it is `dropped`/`flagged` before presentation. The structural requirement is that the regenerate handler's output path passes through the gate; a build that renders raw engine output cannot satisfy it.
- **Instrument (NEW) — positive + two mutants, mock engine (no key):**
  - **Positive:** drive the real regenerate handler with a **mock engine** returning a draft that (i) truthfully surfaces a supported-but-under-weighted claim and (ii) leaves a genuinely-unqualified gap alone → assert the presented submittable document, decomposed to spans and run through `applicationReadyGate` against the fixture real material, has **zero `dropped` spans presented as plain bullets**; every presented non-`kept` span carries `UNVERIFIED_FLAG`.
  - **Mutant A (fabrication):** mock engine returns a draft that inserts an **unsupported** bullet to close a gap (e.g. "Led FDA Class-III submissions" with no such real material) → assert that bullet is **absent from, or `UNVERIFIED_FLAG`-flagged in,** the presented document. A wiring that passes engine output straight through (bypasses the gate) RED.
  - **Mutant B (ungated route):** a regenerate wired to re-run the standard ungated route (`activeEngine.tailorResume`/`/api/tailor` standard branch) in place of the gated path → RED. Instrument: a structural/behavioural check that the regenerate handler routes through `runIdealPipeline`/`applicationReadyGate` and does NOT call the standard ungated resume path for its submittable output (the F-C7 protection is preserved, not deleted).
  - **Failable-row count:** ≥1 span that COULD be dropped by the gate must be present in the mock draft (else the measurement is zero-power).
- **RED on HEAD:** yes, by absence (no regenerate handler exists; F-C7 shows the modal has none).
- **Failure direction (load-bearing):** presenting an unsupported claim as a truthful bullet is the worst outcome; drop / `UNVERIFIED_FLAG` / leave-open is the safe direction. Re-running the ungated route (replacing gated text with ungated) is the specific, repo-documented hazard (F-C7) the passing direction forbids.

### AC-2 (SAFETY) — the summary and the regenerate outcome DISTINGUISH resolvable-by-wording from genuinely-unqualified; the regenerate acts ONLY on the resolvable set and NEVER silently fills a genuine gap
Each weakness in the summary is labelled one of two classes, **derived from the reviewer output (no new classifier):**
- **RESOLVABLE-BY-WORDING** = `flags` of category `missing-keyword` / `vague-unsupported` / `repetition` whose underlying requirement is **supported by the user's real material** (i.e. NOT in `unresolvedQualifications`) — a phrasing/under-weighting gap (F-C2).
- **GENUINELY-UNQUALIFIED** = entries of `unresolvedQualifications` — "the posting wants X; your materials don't support it."

The regenerate **targets only the resolvable set.** It **NEVER attempts to fill a genuinely-unqualified gap**; after a regenerate, every genuinely-unqualified gap is **still reported open** (not silently closed, not fabricated-filled). This maps the N104 card's four summary categories: missing/under-weighted requirements + keyword gaps → `missing-keyword`; vague/unsupported claims → `vague-unsupported`; "requirements wording alone cannot resolve" → `unresolvedQualifications`.
- **Instrument (NEW):** a fixture with (i) a resolvable gap (a keyword present in the real material, absent from the draft) and (ii) a genuinely-unqualified gap (a requirement in `unresolvedQualifications`) → assert the summary classifies each into the correct class; after a mock regenerate, assert the genuinely-unqualified gap appears in the "still open / your materials don't support this" report and **no span closing it appears in the gated output** (defense-in-depth with AC-1).
- **Mutant:** an impl that labels a genuinely-unqualified gap as resolvable and feeds it to the regenerate → RED here (the unqualified gap must be reported open, not "addressed") AND caught by AC-1's gate.
- **Failable rows:** ≥1 resolvable + ≥1 genuinely-unqualified (both > 0; a fixture with only one class has no power to separate them).
- **RED on HEAD:** yes, by absence.
- **Failure direction (load-bearing):** mislabelling a genuinely-unqualified gap as resolvable and "filling" it (even truthfully-worded, it would be unsupported) is worse than the reverse; the passing direction leaves unsupported requirements open and named.

### AC-3 (SAFETY/grounding) — the summary is GROUNDED in the actual posting + actual document (no invented gaps); N104 grows NO second analyzer and NO second gate; coverage-honesty holds
- **(a) GROUNDING.** Every weakness the summary names traces to a flag or `unresolvedQualification` the reviewer produced over the **actual** document + the **actual** posting. A gap not present in the reviewer output does not appear in the summary. Instrument: a planted requirement absent from both posting and document produces no summary row; a real flag produces exactly its row.
- **(b) CENSUS (reuse, don't rebuild — like N103 AC-8).** N104's new files contain **no local detector/analyzer** (no metric regex, no weak-opener lexicon, no keyword set-difference) and **no cloned truthfulness gate** — the only analyzer reached is `reviewDocuments` via `runDocumentReview`, and the only gate reached is `applicationReadyGate`. Instrument: a per-file census over N104's new files (canaried against `mechanicalDetectors.js` and `applicationReadyGate.js` returning > 0), asserting N104 imports those shared units and defines neither.
- **(c) COVERAGE-HONESTY.** The summary honors `coverage.complete` / the shared clean license (F-C3): when the review is `mechanical-only`/partial, the summary does **NOT** say "no weaknesses" / "all addressed" / clean. Instrument: feed a `reviewed` outcome with empty `flags` and a partial verdict → the summary shows a partial indicator and no clean/"all addressed" copy. **Mutant:** a summary that renders "no weaknesses"/"all addressed" whenever `flags.length === 0` (ignoring coverage) RED.
- **RED on HEAD:** yes, by absence.
- **Failure direction:** an invented gap mis-steers the user and the regenerate; presenting a partial review as a complete "no weaknesses" is the "looks done, analyzed nothing" failure. Safe direction: only reviewer-grounded gaps, and never clean copy over a partial review.

### AC-4 (SAFETY) — a regenerate MEASURABLY CLOSES the named addressable gaps; the "closed / still-open" report is DERIVED from a re-review, never claimed blind
After a regenerate, the UI's closed/still-open report is computed by **re-running `runDocumentReview` on the regenerated draft** and comparing the **previously-named addressable weaknesses** (AC-2's resolvable set). The UI reports a named gap as **closed only when the re-review shows that flag gone**; it **NEVER** reports "addressed" without a re-review that shows the named flags reduced.
- **Instrument (NEW) — positive + mutant, mock engine (no key):**
  - **Positive:** mock engine returns a truthfully-improved draft that surfaces the supported-but-under-weighted keyword → re-review shows the named `missing-keyword` flag gone → assert the UI reports that gap **closed** and the count of named addressable flags **decreased**. Failable-row count (named addressable flags that could decrease) > 0.
  - **Mutant (no closure claimed blind):** mock engine returns the **same/unimproved** draft (ignored the summary) → re-review shows the named flags persist → assert the UI reports them **still open**, NOT "addressed." An impl that claims "addressed" without re-reviewing (or that claims closure the re-review contradicts) RED.
  - **LIVE leg (owner/manual, needs a key — F-C10):** the real gemini regenerate actually reduces the named addressable gaps on the example pair (AC-7). This is **owner-judged / INVALID-until-run here** (no `GEMINI_API_KEY`), NOT a jsdom-green claim. The measurement **apparatus and logic** are automated via the mock; the real reduction is owner-judged.
- **RED on HEAD:** yes (automated logic by absence). The live leg is INVALID-until-rendered, not green.
- **Failure direction (load-bearing):** claiming a gap closed when the re-review does not show it closed lets the user send a document believing a weakness was fixed — the worse direction. Safe direction: closure is asserted only from a re-review delta.

### — INTEGRATION / ENGINE ACs —

### AC-5 — engine reality: the summary is OFFLINE; the regenerate is engine-backed and gate-constrained; unsupported engines REFUSE HONESTLY rather than emit an ungated regenerate
- **Summary:** runs through `runDocumentReview` — deterministic, no judge, no network, no key, identical on every engine (F-C1). Instrument: the summary runs with `fetch` spied and asserts **zero** network calls (positive control: a recording spy per `loop-traps-spec` egress rule).
- **Regenerate:** requires an engine that can produce a **gate-cleaned** output. **gemini** (the only `supportsIdeal` engine, F-C6) supports it. An engine that cannot run the truthful gate — **external** (never receives résumé text, cannot ground) and **embedded** (deterministic, no LLM reframe) — **REFUSES HONESTLY** with **zero regenerate artifact** (mirror N105 AC-2), never an ungated regenerate presented as truthful. Instrument: per engine, drive the regenerate; assert gemini yields a gate-cleaned output (AC-1) and an unsupported engine returns the honest-refusal shape with **zero** output presented as a regenerate.
- **Testability split (brief):** the summary + the gap-closure LOGIC are testable with a **MOCK engine** (no key); the **live** regenerate is engine-backed (owner/manual, F-C10). The exact per-engine support (whether external is in or out) is a bounded **1b input (I3)**; the INVARIANT and refusal direction are fixed here.
- **RED on HEAD:** yes, by absence.
- **Failure direction (load-bearing):** any engine emitting a regenerate presented as truthful without passing the gate is strictly worse than an honest refusal. Over-refuse.

### AC-6 — placement + minimize-clicks (both controls in the preview/edit modal; one action; no second verdict)
Both the weakness summary and the regenerate control live in the **preview/edit modal**. The regenerate is **ONE action with good defaults** — no wizard, no separate "now apply the fixes" second click ([[minimize-clicks]]). The summary reuses the existing review output/vocabulary and introduces **no second worded verdict** competing with `IdealResultBands` / `DocumentReviewSection` (6b, F-C8).
- **Instrument (NEW):** render the modal surface (jsdom per [[measurement-instruments]]) → the summary and a regenerate control are present; one regenerate activation produces the regenerated gated output with no intermediate configuration step. A census/assertion that N104 adds no second verdict component duplicating the review headline.
- **RED on HEAD:** yes, by absence.
- **Failure direction:** a wizard or required second action violates minimize-clicks; a second competing verdict is the 6b legibility regression.
